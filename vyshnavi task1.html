MONTHLY SALES DATASET TREND

firstly,convert the data column to datetime,calculate monthly sales,convert the period to timestamp for plotting,now plot monthly sales dataset trend.

In [8]:
df['Date'] = pd.to_datetime(df['Date'])
monthly_sales = df.groupby(df['Date'].dt.to_period('M'))['Total Amount'].sum()
monthly_sales.index=monthly_sales.index.to_timestamp()
plt.figure(figsize=(12,5))
plt.plot(monthly_sales.index,monthly_sales.values,marker='o')
plt.title('Monthly Sales Trend')
plt.xlabel('Month')
plt.ylabel('Total Sales')
plt.xticks(rotation=45)
plt.grid(True)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

observation:Here,from the above chart we can observe that the peaks show the highest sale and the decline show the lowest sale

CUSTOMER DEMOGRAPHIC ANLAYSIS

CUSTOMER AGE GROUPS

In [10]:
bins = [0,18,25,35,45,55,65,100]
labels = ['Below 18', '18-25' , '26-35' , '36-45' , '46-55' , '56-65' , 'Above 65']
df['Age Group'] = pd.cut(df['Age'],bins=bins,labels=labels,include_lowest=True)
age_group_counts = df['Age Group'].value_counts().sort_index()
plt.figure(figsize=(10,5))
plt.bar(age_group_counts.index.astype(str),age_group_counts.values)
plt.title('Distribution of Customer by Age Group')
plt.xlabel('Age Group')
plt.ylabel('Number of Customers')
plt.xticks(rotation=30)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

observation:From the above graph the 46-55 age group has the highest number of customers,while customers below 18 and above 65 are very low.overall,most customers are between 26 and 65 years.
